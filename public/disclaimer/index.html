2. F&O Trading Risks

  • Leverage risk: Futures and options are leveraged instruments. Small price moves can cause disproportionately large losses.
  • Time decay: Options lose value every day (Theta). Holding options overnight carries inherent risk.
  • Volatility risk: IV crush after events can destroy option premium even if direction is correct.
  • Liquidity risk: Far OTM options may be illiquid, leading to wide bid-ask spreads.
  • Expiry risk: Options expire worthless if not in-the-money at expiry.
  • Margin risk: Futures positions may face margin calls requiring additional capital.
  • Market risk: Gaps, circuit breakers, and black swan events can cause losses beyond stop-losses.
