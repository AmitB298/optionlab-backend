What are Options? CE & PE Explained

⏱ 8 min read EN 🆓 Free Chapter

Options Kya Hote Hain?

Options ek type ke financial contracts hain jo aapko ek specific price pe ek specific date tak kuch kharidne ya bechne ka adhikar (right) dete hain — obligation nahi.

India mein NSE pe Nifty 50 aur BankNifty ke weekly aur monthly options available hain. Har option contract mein 50 shares (Nifty) ya 15 shares (BankNifty) hote hain — ise lot size kehte hain.

💡 Simple Analogy

Socho ki ek flat ₹50 lakh ka hai. Developer aapko kehta hai: "₹1 lakh do, aur agle 3 mahine mein ₹50 lakh mein kharidne ka adhikar tumhara." Agar flat ki price ₹60 lakh ho gayi, tum ₹50 lakh mein khareed ke ₹10 lakh profit mein bech sakte ho. Agar price nahi badhi, sirf ₹1 lakh jaata hai. Yahi concept options mein hai — aap premium pay karte ho right ke liye, obligation ke liye nahi.

Do Types: CE aur PE

📈 Call Option (CE — Call European)
CE option aapko ek specific price (strike price) pe asset kharidne ka adhikar deta hai, ek specific date (expiry) tak.

Example: Nifty 22,500 CE — aapko Nifty ko 22,500 pe kharidne ka adhikar milta hai. Agar Nifty 23,000 jaata hai, aapka CE valuable ho jaata hai kyunki aap still 22,500 mein khareed sakte ho.
📉 Put Option (PE — Put European)
PE option aapko ek specific price pe asset bechne ka adhikar deta hai.

Example: Nifty 22,500 PE — aapko Nifty ko 22,500 pe bechne ka adhikar milta hai. Agar Nifty 22,000 girta hai, aapka PE valuable ho jaata hai kyunki aap still 22,500 mein bech sakte ho.

Key Terms

Strike Price
Woh fixed price jis pe aap option exercise kar sakte ho. Example: 22,500 CE mein 22,500 strike price hai.
Expiry Date
Woh date jis ke baad option expire ho jaata hai. Nifty ke weekly Thursday expiry hote hain, monthly last Thursday.
Premium
Option ki current market price — yahi aap pay karte ho right ke liye. Premium buyer ke liye cost hai, seller ke liye income.
Lot Size
Ek contract mein kitne underlying units hain. Nifty = 50 units, BankNifty = 15 units. 22,500 CE premium ₹100 hai toh ek lot cost = ₹100 × 50 = ₹5,000.
ITM / ATM / OTM
ITM (In The Money): Strike price has value. ATM (At The Money): Strike ≈ current price. OTM (Out of The Money): Strike has no intrinsic value yet.

Buyer vs Seller

Options mein do sides hoti hain:

Option Buyer (Long): Premium pay karta hai, unlimited profit potential (CE mein), limited risk (sirf premium lost hota hai agar wrong).

Option Seller (Short/Writer): Premium receive karta hai, limited profit (sirf premium), but theoretically higher risk if market moves strongly against the position.

📊 Important Data Point

SEBI ke 2023 study ke anusaar, 90% retail F&O traders ne losses face kiye. Yeh statistic isliye important hai kyunki options ki complexity aur leverage ki wajah se risk management absolutely essential hai. Yeh educational data hai — koi prediction nahi.
