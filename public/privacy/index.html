Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 17, 2026

Short version: We collect your email/phone to create your account and send you login links. We do not sell your data. We do not show you ads. You can delete your account anytime.

1. Who We Are

OptionsLab Technologies operates the website optionslab.in and provides analytical tools for Indian F&O traders. We are NOT registered with SEBI as an Investment Advisor.

Contact: support@optionslab.in

2. Information We Collect

Account information: Email address or mobile number used to create your account and send magic link / OTP for login.

Payment information: We use Razorpay for payments. We do not store your card details — Razorpay handles all payment data under their PCI-DSS compliance.

Usage data: Pages visited, features used, device type, browser, IP address. Used for platform improvement only.

Angel One credentials (optional): If you connect your Angel One account, your API key and client code are stored encrypted in our database. We only use this to fetch market data on your behalf — we never initiate trades.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To send login magic links and OTP
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To fetch market data via Angel One SmartAPI on your behalf
  • To send product updates and important notices (you can unsubscribe)
  • To improve our platform based on usage patterns

We do not: sell your data, show you third-party ads, or share your data with anyone except as described below.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Angel One — market data fetch (only if you connect your account)
  • Railway.app — our backend hosting provider
  • Law enforcement — if legally required by Indian law

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Railway.app servers. Passwords are never stored — we use magic links and OTPs only. Angel One credentials are stored encrypted (AES-256).

We take reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee absolute security. F&O trading data and analytics are not considered sensitive personal data under DPDP Act 2023.

6. Cookies

We use a single httpOnly cookie (ol_tok) to keep you logged in. This is a session authentication cookie — it contains no tracking or advertising data. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data — email support@optionslab.in
  • Correction: Update your profile from the dashboard
  • Deletion: Request account deletion — we will delete all your data within 30 days
  • Portability: Export your data in JSON format on request

8. Children

OptionsLab is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be notified via email. Continued use after changes = acceptance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: support@optionslab.in
