Refund Policy
Last updated: April 17, 2026

✅ 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee — First-time subscribers on Pro or Elite get a full refund within 7 days, no questions asked. Email support@optionslab.in with your registered email and reason (optional).

1. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You are a first-time subscriber on Pro or Elite plan
  • You request the refund within 7 calendar days of payment
  • Your account has not been suspended for ToS violations

Refunds are not available for:

  • Renewals (month 2 onwards)
  • Partial months — we do not prorate
  • Accounts suspended for misuse or ToS violations
  • Downgrade from Elite to Pro (no partial refund)

2. How to Request a Refund

1
Email us within 7 days
Send an email to support@optionslab.in from your registered email address. Subject: "Refund Request — [your email]"
2
We confirm within 24 hours
We'll verify your eligibility and confirm the refund. No questions asked — but feedback is appreciated.
3
Refund processed in 5–7 business days
Razorpay processes the refund to your original payment method. UPI refunds are usually faster (1–3 days). Card refunds may take up to 7 business days depending on your bank.

3. Cancellation (No Refund)

You can cancel your subscription anytime from your profile dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing immediately. You retain access to your current plan until the end of the billing period. No refund is issued for the remaining days of the current period.

4. Failed Payments

If a payment fails due to a bank or gateway error and you were charged, we will issue a full refund within 3 business days. Email us with your Razorpay transaction ID.

5. Disputes

If you initiated a chargeback with your bank before contacting us, we may not be able to process a direct refund until the chargeback is resolved. Please contact us first — we resolve all legitimate refund requests promptly.

6. Contact

Refund requests: support@optionslab.in

Response time: Within 24 hours on business days (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 6 PM IST)
