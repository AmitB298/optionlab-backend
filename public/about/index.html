"The Story Behind OptionsLab"

I was trading Nifty F&O every week and getting blindsided on expiry day. Every tool gave me price data. None showed me where the money was actually sitting.

I watched retail traders — including myself — consistently lose to institutional players who had access to GEX data, dark pool signals, and FII flow analysis that we simply couldn't see.

I spent two years building the data infrastructure to track these signals. OptionsLab is the result — a platform that gives retail F&O traders access to the same types of data that institutional desks use for market analysis.

The goal is simple: give retail traders access to professional-grade analytical data. All tools are educational — OptionsLab is NOT SEBI registered and does not provide investment advice.
